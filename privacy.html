Privacy Policy for Angler's Rest

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Angler's Rest ("the game") is made by Kaan Oner ("we"). This page explains what information the game uses and why.

The short version

  • Your game progress is saved only on your phone. We don't run servers and we don't have accounts.
  • We don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location.
  • The game shows video ads only when you choose to watch one. These ads come from Google AdMob, which may collect some information from your device, as explained below.

Information the game keeps on your phone

The game saves your progress on your device. This includes your catches, coins, items, settings and the player name you choose. This save file never leaves your phone. We can't see it and we don't receive it.

If your phone's Android backup is turned on, Android may include the game's save in your own Google backup. This lets you keep your progress on a new phone. That backup is handled by Google under your Google account, not by us.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded video ads, which you can choose to watch for an in-game reward. To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, which gives an approximate location;
  • basic device and app information, such as the phone model, the Android version and the language;
  • information about how you interact with the ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game first shows Google's consent form. You can change your choice any time in the game: Settings > Privacy > Ad choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the phone).

Google's own policies explain how it uses this information:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can play without ever watching an ad.
  • You can change your ad consent in Settings > Privacy > Ad choices (where it applies).
  • Uninstalling the game deletes its save from your phone.

Changes

If the game changes what it collects, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date.
